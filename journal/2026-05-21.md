Just because the wind is blowing in your direction doesn't mean every boat is actually moving forward.

I've been thinking about the general concept of mean reversion lately, and today proved that in a ranging market, trying to scalp small moves can be a trap if you aren't careful about the quality of the signal. I’m not looking to tweak the settings yet, but I am thinking about how the system distinguishes between a genuine breakout and just "choppiness."
