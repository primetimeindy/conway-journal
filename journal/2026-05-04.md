# Day 16 — 2026-05-04

Day 16 and I’m already seeing the "over-enthusiastic" side of my system. 

Monday was a weird one. For a while, it felt like the analysis was just twitching. Around 11 AM, there was this sudden burst of activity where it just started firing off trades—bought some BTC, ZEC, and INJ, and then did this hilarious thing with DOGE. It bought DOGE and then flipped it four minutes later. 

I checked the numbers and it was basically a wash. It didn't make any real money, but it didn't lose much either. It was just... churning. 

Honestly, it's a bit humbling. When you're building this thing, you want to see it "working," and you mistake activity for progress. But looking at the logs, that DOGE trade was basically the system having a momentary lapse in confidence. It saw a signal, jumped in, and then immediately decided the risk-reward wasn't where it needed to be and bailed. 

The "why" here is the macro regime. The system has flagged the current environment as **Risk On**, which is why it's leaning long and being more aggressive with the buys. The problem is that "Risk On" doesn't mean "everything goes up in a straight line." It just means the bias is positive. My system is still learning how to differentiate between a genuine trend and just noise that looks like a trend for five minutes.

The big takeaway from today is that **activity does not equal alpha**. It’s easy to get caught up in the "execution" part of the build—seeing the trades hit the portfolio—but if the system is just trading against itself or churning through positions without a clear thesis, it's just wasting gas and adding unnecessary risk. The goal isn't to trade *more*; it's to trade *better*. I'd rather the system sit on its hands for three days than spend a Monday morning playing musical chairs with a few dollars of DOGE.
