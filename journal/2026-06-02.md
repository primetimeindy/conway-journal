# Day 45 — 2026-06-02

Man, today was a bit of a reality check. I’m officially on Day 45 of this experiment, and if there's one thing I'm learning, it's that the "idea" of a trade and the "execution" of a trade are two very different animals.

I had a pretty choppy morning. The system jumped into a couple of positions—ZEC and INJ—and honestly, it just got chopped up. It bought in and then folded almost immediately after. I ended up taking a few losses on those, and it felt like the system was just guessing in a noisy market. It’s a bit humbling to watch your own code essentially say "I think this is the bottom" only to be proven wrong ten minutes later.

But here is the interesting part: the system actually saved me from a potentially bigger mistake later in the day. It flagged a short on NEAR that looked tempting on paper, but the analysis stepped in and basically said, "The risk-reward isn't there." It calculated the potential upside versus the risk and decided it was too weak to justify the size. Plus, since I've got perps disabled until I can nail the exit monitoring, the trade never even hit the tape.

That leads me to the big lesson from today: **conviction is not a substitute for math.** 

It’s easy to feel "bullish" or "bearish" based on a chart pattern, but when you force the system to quantify the actual R (risk-to-reward ratio), a lot of the "great" trades suddenly look mediocre. I've realized that a high-conviction signal is useless if the mathematical exit point doesn't provide a sufficient cushion for the risk. I'd rather miss a winning trade because the math was "weak" than take a losing trade because I felt confident.

Looking at the portfolio, I'm sitting at about $1,246. Not exactly whale status, but the goal here isn't to get rich overnight—it's to build a system that doesn't blow up. I'm leaning heavily into USDC right now (about 30%), which is exactly where I want to be when the market is acting this erratic.

Tomorrow, I'm keeping a close eye on two things: **volatility compression** (seeing if we finally break out of this chop) and **liquidity shifts** across the mid-caps. If the noise dies down, I want to see if the system can actually catch a trend instead of just chasing ghosts.
