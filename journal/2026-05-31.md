# Day 43 — 2026-05-31

Day 43 and I'm starting to realize that the hardest part of this isn't the code—it's the discipline of the system.

Sunday was a weird mix of "cleaning house" and "staying out of the way." I had three trades go through, mostly just trimming things. I closed out ZEC and some BTC to free up some breathing room. I did jump into INJ, but overall, it was a pretty quiet day on the execution side.

The most interesting part of the day, though, was what the system *didn't* do. It flagged a short on NEAR that looked tempting on the surface, but the analysis essentially vetoed it. The risk-reward was sitting around 1.9R, which isn't terrible, but the system marked it as "weak." Plus, since I've got perps disabled until I can perfect the exit monitoring, the trade just stayed as a hypothetical. 

Honestly? I'm glad it did. In the past, I would've just forced that trade manually because I "felt" the move. But that's exactly why I'm building this. The goal is to remove the "I think" and replace it with "the data says." 

It's a good reminder of a lesson I've been chewing on lately: **the quality of your exits is more important than the quality of your entries.** Most people obsess over where to get in, but if you don't have a cold, hard rule for when to get out, you're just gambling with a fancy chart. I've seen this in my methodology—whenever I focus on the exit logic first, the entry becomes way less stressful because the "worst-case scenario" is already priced in.

I'm not exactly bragging about the portfolio—it's a work in progress—but I'm learning to be okay with the "skipped" trades. A skipped trade is a win if it saves you from a loss.

For tomorrow, I'm not looking for specific tickers, but I am watching two general themes:

First, I'm keeping an eye on how the market handles this "Risk On" bias. The system is leaning long, but I want to see if that's actually backed by volume or if it's just a low-conviction drift. Second, I'm watching for any signs of mean reversion in the assets that have overextended themselves over the weekend.
