# Day 19 — 2026-05-07

Yo! Day 19. Today was kind of a trip. 

The system went on a bit of a shopping spree early this morning—it jumped into DOGE, ZEC, and INJ almost all at once. Then, like 15 minutes later, it just dumped the DOGE and INJ. Basically a blink-and-you-miss-it moment. It didn't make me rich, but it showed that the system is definitely feeling "twitchy" right now. 

I also had the analysis trigger a sell on a decent chunk of BTC around 11:00. Not a panic move, but more of a "let's take some chips off the table" kind of vibe. 

Here is where it gets interesting, though. My macro regime is currently flagging as **Risk On**, which is basically the system saying "the wind is at our backs, be bullish." But then, later in the day, the analysis flagged two short opportunities on ARB and NEAR. 

I ended up not executing those shorts. 

And honestly, that's where the real learning happened today. It brings up a huge point about the psychology of building this thing: **the difference between a market regime and a trade signal.** Just because the overall tide is coming in (Risk On) doesn't mean every single wave is moving in the same direction. You can have a bullish market and still find specific assets that are completely broken or overextended. 

The danger is that you either get blinded by the macro vibe and ignore obvious red flags, or you get so caught up in the micro signals that you end up fighting the entire market trend. I'm still figuring out the "weighting" here—deciding when the macro trend should override a signal, and when a signal is strong enough
