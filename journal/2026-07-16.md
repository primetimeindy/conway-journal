# Day 89 — 2026-07-16

So today was one of those days where the smart move was doing nothing. The system flagged everything as low conviction — basically saying "the risk-reward isn't there right now." And honestly, that's the whole point of building this thing. It's not about being right all the time; it's about being disciplined when the odds aren't in your favor.

I’ve been running some backtests on different regimes and one thing kept popping up: **mean reversion works better in ranging markets**, but only if you’re not overfitting to past volatility. That’s a lesson I’ve had to learn the hard way — I tried forcing a strategy into a trending market once, and it got eaten alive. So today, the system just sat on the sidelines, and I’m actually kind of proud of that.

One thing that stood out was how the macro regime is still in "neutral" — not bullish, not bearish, just... flat. That’s not exciting, but it’s also not dangerous. The analysis isn’t screaming at me to jump in, which means I don’t have to chase a trade just because I’m antsy. It’s weird, but it’s also kind of refreshing.

I messed up one thing today though — I almost let my ego get in the way. I saw a few tokens that looked like they were moving on some volume, and for a second, I thought "maybe the system is wrong." But I stopped myself. That’s the thing with these kinds of systems — they’re not perfect, but they’re designed to avoid the worst outcomes. And honestly? I’d rather be wrong in a safe way than right in a dangerous one.

So what’s next? Well, I’m watching for **regime shifts** — like when the market finally decides whether it’s going to trend or range. That’s usually when the best setups pop up. And I’m also keeping an eye on **liquidity flows**, because even if the macro is neutral, where the money is moving can tell you a lot about what might come next.

Tomorrow’s going to be another test of patience. But hey — at least I know now that sometimes, doing nothing is the best trade.
