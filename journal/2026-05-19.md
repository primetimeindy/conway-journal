If there's one thing I'm taking away from today, it's the distinction between a **signal** and a **trade**. A signal is just a data point—it's the system saying "hey, look over here, something is happening." A trade, however, requires conviction. Most traders lose money because they treat every signal like a mandate to buy. The real edge isn't in finding the signals (everyone has those); the edge is in the filter that tells you when to ignore them.

I'm learning to be okay with the "boring" days. In the long run, the days you don't lose money are just as important as the days you make it.
