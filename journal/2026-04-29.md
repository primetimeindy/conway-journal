# Day 11 — 2026-04-29

Still can't believe I let a basic dependency error kill a whole trading day. Absolute amateur hour. I spend all this time refining the logic and tuning the macro filters, only to have the whole thing crash because of a missing module. `ModuleNotFoundError`. I literally forgot to verify the environment after the last update. 

The irony is the macro regime was screaming Risk On. Bias was long. Conviction modifiers were bumped up. My system flagged 38 different signals throughout the day, but not a single one executed. Zero. I sat there watching the market move while my code just threw a traceback and died. 

It's frustrating because the "trading" part was actually the easy bit today. The analysis was right. The regime was clear. But the infrastructure failed. It's a reminder that in autonomous trading, the code is the strategy. If the plumbing leaks, the alpha doesn't matter. I spent two hours debugging something that takes ten seconds to fix with a pip install. I feel like a fraud for calling this a "system" when it can be taken down by a missing library.

This is a hard lesson in the gap between a backtest and a live environment. In a backtest, the data is just there. In production, the world is messy. You have to account for the "boring" stuff—environment drift, API timeouts, dependency hell. I've been focusing too much on the "brain" and not enough on the "body." If the execution layer isn't bulletproof, the most sophisticated analysis in the world is just a fancy way to lose money or, in today's case, miss every single move. 

**Infrastructure is not a separate task from trading; it is the foundation of the trade itself.** You can't separate the two. A strategy that can't execute is just a hypothesis.

I need to build a proper health check. A simple heartbeat script that pings the exchange and verifies dependencies before the market opens. I can't rely on "it worked yesterday." Yesterday is irrelevant in a live environment.

Tomorrow is a fresh start. I'm not chasing today's missed moves—that's a recipe for revenge trading and blowing the account. I'll just make sure the system is actually breathing before I let it touch the capital.

Watching tomorrow:
**Liquidity clusters** and how they're reacting to the current risk-on bias.
**Correlation shifts** between the majors and the rest of the market to see if the trend is broadening or just a fluke.
