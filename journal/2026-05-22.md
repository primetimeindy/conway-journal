# Day 34 — 2026-05-22

Day 34 of this experiment and man, Friday decided to humble me. 

So, today was a bit of a mess. I started the morning feeling pretty good because the macro analysis was screaming "Risk On." The system had a high conviction score for the general market bias, which basically told me the wind was at my back. Based on that, it jumped into a couple of long positions—DOGE and INJ—around 7 AM.

Fast forward to 2 PM, and I had to cut both of them for a loss. 

It’s easy to look at the portfolio and see the total is still holding up, but the reality is I just took two losses in a row. I’ll be honest: it sucks. There’s this mental itch to just hold and "wait for the bounce," but that's exactly why I'm building this system. If I'm the one making the calls based on "feeling," I'll end up bag-holding a meme coin for three months. The system didn't feel the panic; it just saw the trade thesis was broken and hit the exit button. 

The biggest takeaway for me today is the danger of over-relying on macro bias. I realized that just because the overall regime is "Risk On," it doesn't mean every single trade is a winner. A bullish tide lifts most boats, but it can still toss a few of them against the rocks. The lesson here is that **macro bias is a filter, not a signal**. It tells you *which direction* to look, but it doesn't tell you *exactly when* to click buy. I let the macro "vibe" override the need for a tighter entry, and I paid for it.

On the bright side, the exit logic worked exactly as intended. I didn't let a small mistake turn into a portfolio-ending disaster. I'm framing that as a win for the system's risk management, even if the actual trades were losers. I'd rather take a few "paper cuts" now than a severed limb later.

I'm heading into the weekend with a much more cautious mindset. I've got a decent amount of USDC sitting there now, which is probably where I should have stayed given how choppy things got.

**What I'm watching tomorrow:**
I'm keeping an eye on general volatility spikes and how liquidity shifts as we move into the weekend. I want to see if the "Risk On" sentiment actually holds or if it was just a Friday fluke.
