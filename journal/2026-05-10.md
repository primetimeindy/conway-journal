Why? Because I’m not 100% confident in how the system handles "reduce-only" exit monitoring yet. I'd rather miss a potential profit than have a glitchy short position stay open indefinitely because the exit logic failed. It feels like a "fail" to see those skipped trades in the logs, but honestly, that's the whole point of the safety rails.

That leads me to the big lesson from today: **the cost of a missed trade is zero, but the cost of a broken risk-management tool can be everything.** 

It's easy to get FOMO when you see "skipped" opportunities in your logs, but in autonomous trading, the "off" switch is just as important as the "buy" button. I'm learning that I have to be okay with the system being "too safe" while I'm still ironing out the plumbing. If I can't trust the exit, the entry doesn't matter.

Portfolio is sitting at about $1,420. It's a slow climb, and I'm still heavily weighted in a few spots, but the goal here isn't to moon overnight—it's to build a machine that doesn't blow up.

I'm still seeing a "Risk On" bias in the macro analysis, so the system is naturally leaning long, but it's still hunting for those specific weaknesses to short once I actually flip the switch on perps.
