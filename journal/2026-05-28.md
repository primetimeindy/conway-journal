# Day 40 — 2026-05-28

Day 40 of this experiment and I'm starting to realize that the hardest part of building a system isn't the code—it's the discipline of the guardrails.

Today was actually a pretty decent day on the execution side. The system played it relatively safe with a few spot entries in the morning. I ended up closing out ZEC and INJ for a small profit. Nothing that's going to make me retire early, but it's a win because it proves the exit logic is actually firing when the targets are hit. 

But here is where it gets interesting. Later in the day, the analysis started flagging some short opportunities on ARB and NEAR. If I were trading manually, I might have just jumped in. But the system skipped them entirely. Why? Because I have perps (futures) disabled until I can get the "reduce-only" exit monitoring perfectly dialed in. 

It felt like a missed opportunity in the moment, but honestly, it's a huge win for the project. The biggest mistake most people make when building a bot is letting it trade "mostly" correctly. If the exit logic isn't 100% bulletproof, a single glitch can wipe out a month of gains. I'd rather miss ten good trades than take one trade that the system doesn't know how to close.

The real lesson from today: **operational safety is more important than signal accuracy.** You can have a 90% win rate, but if your risk management module has a bug, that win rate is irrelevant. I'm learning to trust the "skipped" trades as much as the "executed" ones.

Looking at the portfolio, I'm still heavily weighted in MON, which is carrying a lot of the load right now. I need to keep an eye on that concentration risk as I scale.

As for tomorrow, I'm watching two main themes:

First, I'm looking at the tension between the current "Risk On" macro regime and the fact that the system is starting to lean toward short-term bearish signals on a few assets. I want to see if the system catches a regime shift or if these are just blips in an uptrend.

Second, I'm focusing on the "spot-only" constraint. I want to see if the system can find high-conviction long setups that fit the current bias without needing the leverage of perps.
