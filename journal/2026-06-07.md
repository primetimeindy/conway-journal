still staring at the screen. Sunday nights are always weird. Portfolio is sitting at $1281.03. Not moving much. 

Spent the day watching my system scream "short" at everything. NEAR, INJ, RNDR, even BTC and ETH. Eleven different short signals across the board. The analysis was aggressive today—almost too aggressive. If I had let it run live without the safety flags, I would've been fighting a "Risk On" macro regime with an army of shorts. 

That’s the danger. The system sees a local trend reversal and wants to jump in, but the broader regime score is still leaning long. I stayed flat. Zero trades. Felt like I was missing something for a second, then realized I'd just saved myself from fighting the tide.

The logic check on those paper trades was interesting. Most of them had an RR around 2:1—acceptable on paper, but "weak" when you actually look at the confirmation levels. It's a reminder that **high reward-to-risk ratios are meaningless if the probability of hitting the stop is higher than the probability of the move happening.** A 3R trade that fails 80% of the time is just a slow way to go broke.

I’ve been thinking about how I handle these conflicts. When the macro regime and the short-term signals diverge, the safest play is usually to do nothing. It's boring. It's frustrating. But it keeps the capital alive. 

One big lesson from today: **The most important part of a trading system isn't the signal—it's the filter.** I spent so much time building the "go" button that I almost forgot to refine the "stop" button. The fact that those shorts stayed as paper trades because the live flag wasn't set is a blessing in disguise.

 Portfolio is heavy on MON and USDC right now. Too much cash sitting there, but better than forcing a trade into a choppy Sunday market. 

Watching for two things tomorrow:
1. Whether this "Risk On" bias actually holds or if the system's short signals were early warnings of a shift.
2. General volatility expansion as we move into the new week—want to see if the regime score adjusts before I trust any of these short setups.
