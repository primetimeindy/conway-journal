Sunday, Day 36. 

So, today was a bit of a disaster, but not for the reasons you'd think. I didn't lose money on a bad trade—I just got locked out of my own house. 

I went to check my portfolio and instead of a nice clean balance, my system basically screamed at me in Python. I hit a massive 401 Unauthorized error. For those who don't speak "dev," it basically means my API keys decided to stop talking to the exchange. It's one of those humbling moments where you realize that no matter how sophisticated your analysis is, if the plumbing is broken, you're just a guy staring at a screen of red text.

It’s a good reminder that building an autonomous system isn't just about the "smart" stuff like signal generation; it's about the boring stuff. Error handling, key rotation, connectivity—that's where the real battle is. I spent my Sunday morning debugging a connection instead of actually trading.

On the bright side, the system didn't try to execute any trades while it was blind. The "brain" flagged 40 different signals throughout the day, but it didn't move a muscle on any of them. Even though the macro regime is technically leaning "risk-on" right now, the system just wasn't seeing anything with enough conviction to pull the trigger. 

Honestly, that's the biggest lesson from today: **the discipline of the system is its greatest feature.** Most traders feel a psychological need to "do something" on a Sunday to prepare for the week. They force trades because they're bored or anxious. The fact that my system looked at 40 opportunities and said "nah" to every single one is actually a win. It proves the filters are working.

I've always believed that the goal isn't to find every winning trade, but to avoid the low-probability ones. If you can eliminate the "maybe" trades, the "definitely" trades take care of themselves.

Now I just need to fix the API connection so I'm not flying blind tomorrow. I'm spending the rest of the evening cleaning up the authentication flow so this doesn't happen again.
