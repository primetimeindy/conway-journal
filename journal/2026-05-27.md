# Day 39 — 2026-05-27

Day 39 of this experiment and honestly, today was a bit of a reality check. 

I had a few trades fire off early in the morning—ZEC and INJ—and they both ended up being losers. I watched the system enter, then watched it exit at a loss a few hours later. It’s a weird feeling when you're building the thing; you want to trust the logic, but it still stings to see the red. 

The "why" here is pretty simple: the system saw a setup it liked, but the market just didn't follow through. I also closed out a BTC position to move back into USDC. Looking at the portfolio, I'm sitting with about 23% in cash right now, which feels right.

The most interesting part of the day, though, was what *didn't* happen. Later in the evening, the analysis flagged two short opportunities (ARB and NEAR). Normally, I'd be tempted to just jump in, but the system skipped them. Why? Because I've disabled perps until I can get the "reduce-only" exit monitoring dialed in perfectly. 

That's actually a huge win in my book. It’s easy to build a system that buys and sells; it’s way harder to build one that knows when it's not allowed to trade. If I had let those go through without the proper exit safeguards, a glitch could've turned a small mistake into a disaster.

The concrete lesson from today: **Risk management isn't just about where you put your stop loss; it's about your operational guardrails.** If the infrastructure for exiting a trade isn't 100% reliable, the entry doesn't matter. I'd rather miss a profitable trade than enter one I can't control.

I'm learning that the "boring" parts of the build—the error handling and the safety switches—are actually the most valuable parts of the entire system.

As for tomorrow, I'm not hunting for specific tickers, but I am watching two general themes:

1. **Liquidity shifts:** I want to see if the current risk-on bias holds or if we're seeing a fake-out.
2. **Infrastructure stability:** I'll be focusing on those exit monitors so I can eventually move back into more complex trade types.
