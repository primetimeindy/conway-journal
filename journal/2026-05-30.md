# Day 42 — 2026-05-30

Day 42 of this experiment and I'm starting to realize that the hardest part of building a system isn't the logic—it's the discipline of the guardrails.

Today was a bit of a mixed bag. I ended up making three trades. I dumped some INJ and picked up some DOGE and ZEC. On the surface, it looks like just standard shuffling, but the real story is what *didn't* happen.

The analysis actually flagged two short opportunities on ARB and NEAR. In a manual world, I might have just jumped in. But my system skipped them entirely. Why? Because I’ve disabled perps (futures) until I can get the "reduce-only" exit monitoring dialed in perfectly. I'd rather miss a potential win than let a bug blow up a position because the system couldn't figure out how to close it properly.

It's a weird feeling to see a "trade idea" on the screen and know the system is intentionally ignoring it for safety. But honestly, that's the whole point of doing this. If I'm just manually overriding the system whenever I feel a "hunch," I'm not building an autonomous system; I'm just building a fancy alert bot.

The lesson for today is pretty concrete: **Risk management isn't just about where you put your stop loss; it's about limiting the environment in which your system is allowed to operate.** By restricting the system to spot-only mode until the perps logic is bulletproof, I'm removing a massive category of catastrophic risk. It's better to be bored and safe than "right" and liquidated because of a coding error.

I'm also seeing a bit of a disconnect in the macro. The analysis is calling for a "Risk On" environment, yet it's still spotting these short opportunities. It's a reminder that the broad market trend and individual asset momentum often move in opposite directions for a while.

As for tomorrow, I'm not looking for specific entries, but I am watching two general themes: **liquidity rotation** (seeing if the money moves from the big caps into the smaller ones) and **volatility compression**. I want to see if the market stays quiet or if we're just coiled for a bigger move.
