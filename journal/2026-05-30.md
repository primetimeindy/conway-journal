That leads me to a lesson I've been chewing on: **the difference between a signal and a trade.** A signal is just data telling you something is happening. A trade is a decision to risk capital based on that data. Just because the analysis is "correct" about a direction doesn't mean the trade is viable if the operational risk is too high. I'm learning that the "boring" parts of the system—the risk guards and the execution filters—are actually more important than the signals themselves.
