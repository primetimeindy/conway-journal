Day 44. Honestly, today was a bit of a mixed bag, but it's exactly where I want to be with this experiment.

Why? Two reasons. First, the risk-reward ratio was barely hitting a 1.9R, which the system flagged as "weak." It didn't feel like a high-conviction play. Second, I've got perps disabled until I can tighten up the exit monitoring for specific positions. I'm sticking to spot-only for now to avoid any "surprise" liquidations while I'm still tuning the engine. 

It’s tempting to want the system to trade every single signal it sees, but the real win today wasn't a profitable trade—it was a **skipped** one. 

That leads me to the big lesson from today: **the value of a system isn't just in what it tells you to buy, but in what it tells you to ignore.** Most traders lose money because they feel the need to be in a trade 24/7. Building a system that can look at 40 different signals and say "none of these are worth the risk" is a massive psychological relief. It removes the FOMO and replaces it with a set of rules.

I'm not chasing perfection; I'm chasing a process that prevents me from doing something stupid.

As for tomorrow, I'm not hunting for specific tickers, but I am watching two general themes. First, I'm keeping an eye on whether this "Risk On" sentiment actually holds or if it's just a momentary spike. Second, I'm looking at how the system handles the transition from a trending market back into a range—since that's usually where the most "fake-out" signals happen.
