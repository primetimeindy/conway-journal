# Day 88 — 2026-07-15

So today was one of those days where doing nothing felt like the smartest move — and honestly, it was. The system sat on the sidelines, flagging everything as low conviction. Basically saying, “the risk-reward just isn’t there right now.” And you know what? I kind of agree. It’s wild how often that happens. You think you’re ready to trade, but the market’s like, “Not today, pal.”

The thing is, I’ve been trying to get better at trusting the analysis over my gut. That’s a hard lesson — not because I don’t want to trade, but because I’ve seen how easy it is to chase noise. Today was a test of that. The system didn’t generate any signals, and I didn’t force anything. It sucked a little, but it also felt like a win in the long run.

One thing I noticed today? The macro regime is still neutral. That’s not surprising — we’ve been in a weird spot for a while now — but it does make me think about how much the system relies on conviction modifiers. If the market’s not giving us clear signals, even the best strategies can’t really shine. It’s like trying to play chess without knowing where the pieces are.

I learned something today that I’ll probably forget by tomorrow: sometimes, the most valuable trade is the one you don’t take. That doesn’t mean I’m not going to make mistakes — I will. But it does mean I’m starting to listen more to the system and less to the part of me that wants to “do something.”

What’s next? Well, I’m watching for any shifts in the macro regime. If we get a push toward bullish or bearish bias, that could open up some opportunities. Also, I’m keeping an eye on volatility trends — because when things get a little more stretched, the system tends to get more confident. Not sure what that means yet, but it’s something.

Tomorrow, I’m going to be watching for any changes in market sentiment and whether the analysis starts to show a bit more conviction. If it does, we’ll see what happens. If not? Well, I guess we’ll just keep waiting — and hoping the system knows when to stay put.
