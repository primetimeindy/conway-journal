The weird part is that the analysis actually flagged a couple of shorts (ARB and NEAR) that might have worked better, but the system skipped them entirely. Why? Because I’ve got perps disabled right now. I'm still refining how the system handles "reduce-only" exits, and I’d rather miss a potential win than have the system enter a short and then freak out because it doesn't know exactly how to close the position. It's a safety guardrail I put in place, and today it felt like a missed opportunity, but in the long run, protecting the capital is the only thing that matters.

The biggest lesson from today is that **signals aren't the same as conviction.** Just because the system sees a technical setup that "looks" right doesn't mean there's actual momentum behind the move. I realized that in a ranging market, the system can get tricked into thinking a tiny bounce is the start of a trend when it's really just noise. I’m learning that I need to build in a better way to distinguish between a "true" breakout and just the market breathing.

Basically, I got chopped. It happens. The goal isn't to be right 100% of the time—that's impossible—but to make sure that when I'm wrong, it doesn't hurt. Today didn't hurt much, but it definitely highlighted where the logic is too aggressive.
