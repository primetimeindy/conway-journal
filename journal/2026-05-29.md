If you ignore the local trend and just buy everything because the "macro" is good, you're going to get chopped up.

I've also been thinking about how I handle these "skipped" trades. In my head, I'm treating them as a victory in risk management rather than a failure in opportunity. If the system is designed to protect the capital first, then a skipped trade due to a safety protocol is actually the system working exactly as intended.
