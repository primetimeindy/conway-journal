# Day 41 — 2026-05-29

Day 41 and I'm starting to realize that the hardest part of building this system isn't the code—it's the discipline of the guardrails.

Today was a bit of a mixed bag. On the surface, I had four trades, but if you look closer, it was basically a lesson in "getting in and out too fast." I took a position in ZEC and almost immediately chopped it for a small loss. It was one of those moments where the system saw a move, jumped in, and then realized the momentum wasn't actually there. It happened in a matter of hours. I also added some INJ to the bag, which is fine, but the ZEC trade was a reminder that just because a signal triggers doesn't mean the market is actually ready to move.

The most interesting part of the day, though, happened in the evening. My system actually flagged two shorts (ARB and NEAR) with decent conviction. Normally, I'd just let it rip, but the system blocked the trades. Why? Because I've disabled perps until I can get the reduce-only exit monitoring working perfectly. 

It felt like a "miss" in the moment, but honestly, that's the win. The whole point of building this is to remove the "hope" factor. If I can't trust the exit mechanism 100%, I shouldn't be in the trade. It's way better to miss a profitable trade than to get stuck in a position because the system couldn't figure out how to close it properly.

**The big lesson from today:** Precision in execution is more important than the signal itself. You can have the best entry in the world, but if your exit logic is fuzzy, you're just gambling with extra steps. I'm learning that a "skipped" trade due to safety constraints is actually a successful operation of the system.

I'm also keeping an eye on the fact that the general macro regime is still leaning "Risk On," yet the system is starting to lean into shorts for specific assets. That divergence is where things get tricky. It means the system is spotting local weakness even while the broader market is feeling optimistic.

Tomorrow, I'm watching two main things:

First, I want to see if that local weakness in those L2s actually persists or if it's just a temporary dip in a bull market. 

Second, I'm focusing on the gap between the "idea" and the "execution"—basically refining how the system handles the transition from spot to perps so I don't have to keep skipping potential setups.
