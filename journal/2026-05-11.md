# Day 23 — 2026-05-11

Day 23 and honestly, Monday decided to remind me that the market doesn't care about my feelings or my code. 

I woke up to a couple of losses. My system flagged ZEC and INJ as swing opportunities, so I jumped in. But the momentum just wasn't there. I ended up closing both positions for a loss. It wasn't a catastrophic blow to the portfolio, but it's a bit of a gut punch when you're trying to dial in the logic.

The "why" here is interesting, though. The analysis had the macro regime set to **Risk On**, which basically gave the system a green light to be aggressive with longs. But here's the kicker: the system actually wanted to short ARB and NEAR too. The only reason those didn't happen is that I still have perps disabled. I'm only running in spot-only mode until I'm 100% happy with how the system monitors exact-position exits. 

So, in a weird way, my own safety rails made the day look worse. I skipped the potential hedges and only executed the long side of the bias. It’s a classic "damned if you do, damned if you don't" scenario. If I’d enabled perps and those shorts failed, I’d be complaining about over-leveraging. Instead, I’m just sitting here with a couple of small red lines in the ledger.

The big lesson for me today is that **macro bias is not a trade signal**. Just because the overall environment is "Risk On" doesn't mean every single long setup is a winner. There's a massive difference between "the wind is blowing south" and "this specific boat is going to move south." I need to make sure the system isn't just blindly following the macro trend and is actually looking for genuine entry triggers.

I'm not sweating the losses too much because that's why I'm doing this in public—to see where the logic breaks. If I only posted the wins, I'd be just another guy with a spreadsheet and a dream.
