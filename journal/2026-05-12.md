# Day 24 — 2026-05-12

Yo, day 24 of this experiment. Honestly, today was a bit of a reality check on the difference between a "good signal" and an actual "tradable setup."

First off, the portfolio is sitting at about $1,380. Not a massive jump, but I'm more focused on the plumbing right now than the PnL. 

The analysis had the macro regime set to **Risk On**, so the system was naturally biased toward longs. It pulled the trigger on ZEC and INJ this morning. The ZEC trade is still hanging out there, but INJ was a total disaster. It bought in, the price immediately dipped, and the system dumped it a few hours later for a loss. 

It’s easy to get annoyed when a trade fails, but that’s actually the point. If I’m building something to be autonomous, I have to trust the exit logic as much as the entry logic. I’d rather take a small, disciplined loss now than hold a bag for three weeks because I’m too proud to admit the analysis was wrong.

The more interesting part of the day, though, was what *didn't* happen. My system actually flagged some short opportunities on ARB and NEAR, but it skipped them entirely. Why? Because I currently have perps disabled and the system is in spot-only mode. 

This leads to the big lesson from today: **an analysis is only as good as its execution environment.** You can have the most accurate signal in the world, but if your account permissions, liquidity, or venue settings aren't aligned with the trade type, that signal is effectively zero. I’m learning that "building the system" isn't just about the math of the trade; it's about the operational constraints of the account it's running on.

I also cleared out some BTC and DOGE to keep the USDC balance healthy. I'm trying to avoid getting over-leveraged in any one direction, even when the bias is long.

It's a slow grind, but seeing the system automatically cut a losing trade without me having to emotionally wrestle with it is a win in my book. That's the whole reason I'm doing this—to remove the "hope" factor from the equation.

As for tomorrow, I'm not looking for specific coins, but I am watching two general themes: **volatility contraction** (waiting to see if things tighten up before a bigger move) and **liquidity rotations** (seeing if the "Risk On" sentiment is actually moving into smaller caps or just idling in the majors). 

Catch you tomorrow.
