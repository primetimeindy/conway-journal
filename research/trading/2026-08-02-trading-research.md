## 1) Crypto momentum with regime filters

This is the best fit for small accounts because you can trade fractional sizes, the market is open 24/7, and momentum/trend-following is one of the few systematic effects that can survive small-account friction if you keep turnover low. The key is to *only trade momentum when the regime is favorable*.

**Practical system**
- **Universe:** Top 20–50 liquid spot pairs on a major exchange, excluding stablecoin pairs.
- **Time horizon:** 1 day to 2 weeks.
- **Regime filter:** Trade only when:
  - BTC is above its **200-day moving average** on the daily chart, and
  - BTC 20-day realized volatility is not in the top decile of the last year, and
  - market breadth is positive, e.g. at least 60% of your universe is above its 50-day MA.
- **Entry rule:** Buy the strongest 3–5 names by **3-month return** or **20-day return** after a shallow pullback:
  - price closes back above the **10-day EMA**, or
  - a breakout above the prior 20-day high with volume above the 20-day average.
- **Exit rule:**
  - time stop at **10 trading days**,
  - or exit on a close below the **20-day EMA**,
  - or a trailing stop at **2.5× ATR(14)**.
- **Position sizing:**
  - risk **0.5%–1.0%** of account equity per position,
  - cap total concurrent risk at **2%–3%**,
  - in a $100 account that means roughly $0.50–$1.00 risk per trade, which often forces very small notional sizes; in practice, that means fewer positions and longer holding periods.
- **Implementation note:** This is easiest on spot, not leveraged perpetuals, because leverage can make small accounts blow up on normal volatility.

**What to avoid**
- Pure breakout chasing in *bear or choppy regimes*.
- High-frequency scalping.
- Leveraged perp funding when funding is persistently positive and crowded.

**Degradation risk**
- The crowded part is the simple “buy high beta altcoins on every risk-on day” crowd. That style tends to degrade when BTC dominance rises or when funding becomes expensive, because many participants are doing the same thing.

## 2) Equity pair trading

For small accounts, pair trading is only actionable if your broker offers **fractional shares** or low commissions and if you keep the pair selection very selective. The most practical version is *sector-relative mean reversion* rather than academic stat-arb with dozens of pairs.

**Practical system**
- **Universe:** Highly liquid U.S. stocks within the same industry, ideally ETFs or mega-caps.
- **Time horizon:** 3 days to 4 weeks.
- **Pair selection:** Choose pairs with:
  - high historical correlation,
  - stable cointegration or at least stable spread behavior,
  - similar catalyst exposure.
- **Entry rule:**
  - compute a spread \(z\)-score over **60 trading days**,
  - enter when spread reaches **|z| ≥ 2.0**,
  - go long the underperformer and short the outperformer,
  - only trade if the sector ETF is neutral or not in a strong trend against you.
- **Exit rule:**
  - take profit at **z = 0.5** or **z = 0**,
  - stop out at **|z| = 3.0
