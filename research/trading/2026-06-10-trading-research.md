## 1) Crypto momentum with regime filters

The most actionable version for a small account is a **trend-following breakout or pullback system on liquid majors** such as BTC and ETH, with a filter that says “trade only when the higher timeframe is trending and volatility is supportive.” The general idea is consistent with the 2026 small-account guidance in your results: trade **pullbacks in trend**, use a **clear stop**, and keep targets realistic rather than chasing huge R-multiples[1][2][4].

A concrete template:

- **Universe:** BTC, ETH, and possibly SOL if liquidity is adequate.
- **Timeframe:** Entry on **4H**, regime on **1D**.
- **Regime filter:** Only take longs when:
  - 20-day EMA is above 50-day EMA on daily chart.
  - Daily close is above the 50-day EMA.
  - 14-day ATR is not collapsing below its 6-month median, because dead volatility tends to produce false breakouts.
- **Entry rule:** Buy a **pullback to the 20-EMA** after an impulse up leg, or buy a breakout above the prior 10-bar high if volume/volatility expands. The “pullback trend strategy” and “breakout” framing is consistent with the 2026 strategy pages you provided[1][2][4].
- **Exit rule:**
  - Hard stop below the most recent swing low or 1.5× ATR below entry, whichever is tighter.
  - First partial at **1R**.
  - Final exit at **2R–3R** or on a close back below the 20-EMA.
- **Position sizing:** Risk **0.5%–1.0% of account per trade**; for a $500 account, that is **$2.50–$5** risk. That’s consistent with the general risk-control guidance in the 2026 sources[2][3].

What makes this actionable is that it doesn’t require prediction; it requires only a robust trend filter and disciplined stops. What makes it fragile is crowding: plain breakout momentum is widely known, and the sources you gave do not contain evidence that this edge has improved recently.

**Degradation flag:**  
- **Moderate degradation risk** in the last 6 months if you’re trading naked breakouts without a regime filter. Breakout strategies are explicitly presented as lower-success-rate in one 2026 summary, and the broader rule-based trend approach is safer than blind momentum chasing[2].
