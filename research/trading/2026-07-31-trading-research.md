- **Crypto momentum with regime filters**
  - **Best use case:** trade only when crypto is in a confirmed *risk-on* regime; avoid chop.
  - **Concrete rules:**
    - **Universe:** BTC, ETH, and the top 10 liquid large caps only.
    - **Time horizon:** 1D swing, hold 3–10 days.
    - **Regime filter:** long-only when BTC is above its 200-day moving average *and* the 20-day return is positive; require ETH/BTC trend confirmation or BTC dominance not rising sharply. This type of trend-plus-regime framework is consistent with the “daily bias / market structure / liquidity / inefficiency” workflow described in the systematic-setup style sources, and with momentum/RS suggestions in retail strategy guides.[17][18][2]
    - **Entry:** buy the first pullback to the 10-day EMA after a breakout to a 20-day high, but only if 3-day realized volatility is below its 30-day median so you are not buying an exhausted spike. The “pullback trend” idea is also aligned with the small-account momentum content in the 2026 retail material.[1][15]
    - **Exit:** take partial profits at +1.5R, trail the rest under the 10-day EMA, and exit if BTC closes back below the 20-day EMA or the 20-day high breakout fails within 3 bars.
    - **Position sizing:** risk **0.5%–1.0% of account per trade**; with $100–$1000, that means roughly $0.50–$10 risk per trade, so use **micro-size** and only trade assets/exchanges with low fees and tight spreads.[3][15]
  - **Degradation flag:** the *fast breakout* crypto momentum trade has likely **degraded** over the last 6 months if you are chasing intraday breakouts; the retail sources emphasize pullbacks and confirmation, which is usually a sign that raw momentum chasing is crowded and less robust.[1][15]

- **Equity pair trading**
  - **Best use case:** only if you can trade **highly liquid pairs** with cheap borrowing/frictions; small accounts should prefer **ETF pairs** or mega-cap pairs.
  - **Concrete rules:**
    - **Universe:** sector twins like XLE/XOP, KO/PEP, or ADBE/CRM; avoid illiquid small caps.
    - **Time horizon:** 3–15 trading days.

    - **Position sizing:** dollar-neutral and *tiny*; size so max spread loss is **0.5%–1% of account**. With $100–$1000, this often means the strategy is only practical with **fractional shares** or ETF proxies.
