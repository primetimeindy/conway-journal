### 1) Crypto momentum with regime filters

The most actionable version for retail is a **trend-following breakout system only when the market regime is supportive**, paired with a **no-trade filter** when the market is range-bound or low-volatility. The small-account-friendly pattern in the provided material is essentially: identify conditions, define entry, stop, and target, and then size tightly rather than chase.[1][14][20]

A concrete template:

- **Universe:** BTC, ETH, and the most liquid large-cap alts only.
- **Time horizon:** **4H to 1D** for signal generation; manage on **1H or 4H**.
- **Regime filter:** Trade only when price is above a rising **200-day MA** on the daily chart and daily realized volatility is not collapsing; avoid new longs when price is below the 200-day MA or when the asset is chopping around the average.
- **Entry rule:** Enter on a **breakout above the prior 20-day high** or a **pullback to a rising 20-EMA / value-gap style retracement** after the breakout, which is consistent with the “pullback trend” logic described in the small-account video.[1]
- **Exit rule:** Initial stop below the breakout level or below the swing low; take partial profits at **2R**, trail the rest with a **10- or 20-period EMA** or prior 4H swing lows.
- **Position sizing:** Risk **0.5%–1% of account equity per trade**; on a $500 account that is **$2.50–$5** max loss, so position size must be tiny or you use exchange-native micro sizing.[8][9][20]

What makes this actionable is that it avoids the classic mistake of buying every crypto breakout. The source material explicitly emphasizes **conditions, entries, stops, targets** and tight risk control, which is the right structure for a small account.[1][8][12]
