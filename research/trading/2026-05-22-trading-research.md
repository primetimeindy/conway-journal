This strategy identifies cryptocurrencies experiencing strong upward momentum when market conditions are favorable.

**Key Takeaways:**
*   **Regime Filters:** Relying solely on momentum breakouts can lead to whipsaws; filtering based on Bitcoin’s performance improves robustness.

**Degradation Warning:** Pure breakout strategies without regime filters have become noisier, and low-cap altcoin momentum is increasingly crowded.

### Equity Pair Trading

Pair trading involves profiting from temporary divergences in historically correlated assets.

**Key Takeaways:**
*   **Correlation Isn't Forever:** Monitor the correlation between assets; it can decay rapidly.
*   **Small Account Friendly:** Use tighter stop-loss orders for smaller accounts.
*   **Regime Shifts:** Be prepared to adjust pairs or exit positions if correlations break down.
