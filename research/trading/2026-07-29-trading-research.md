### 1) Crypto momentum with regime filters

The strongest *retail-implementable* version is not “always-on momentum,” but **momentum only when the market is in risk-on trend regime**.[8][11] The generic rule set most consistent with the supplied sources is:

- **Time horizon:** 1–20 trading days for swing momentum, or 4h/daily if you want fewer decisions.[8][11]
- **Regime filter:** Trade only when price is above the **200-day moving average** or when the broader crypto proxy is above its 50/200-day trend structure; the sources emphasize using moving averages and market context to decide whether a trend or range strategy is appropriate.[4][11]
- **Entry:** Buy pullbacks to the **20 EMA / 50 EMA** in an uptrend, or breakouts above the prior 20-day high after a volume expansion; the educational material highlights pullback trend entries, RVOL, and VWAP/EMA confirmation.[1][11]
- **Exit:** Partial profit at **1.5–2R** and a hard stop below the most recent swing low or a fixed **1R** loss; the small-account guidance repeatedly emphasizes defined risk and 1–2R targets.[2][4][6]
- **Position sizing:** Risk **0.5–1.0% of account equity per trade**; for a $500 account, that means risking **$2.50–$5** per trade, which is consistent with the “risk 1% per trade max” theme in the small-account material.[3][11]
