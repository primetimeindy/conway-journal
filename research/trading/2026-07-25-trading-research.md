- **Crypto momentum with regime filters**
  - **Best use case:** Small spot or perp account where you can trade 1–3 liquid coins and avoid overtrading.
  - **Time horizon:** **4H to 1D** bars, held **2–10 days**.
  - **Universe:** BTC, ETH, and 1–3 high-liquidity large caps only.
  - **Regime filter:** Trade only when price is above a rising **200-day MA** on the daily and **20-day realized volatility** is not collapsing; avoid entries during major event windows or when the market is chopping inside a narrow range. This is consistent with the “trade with the daily trend,” “first 3 hours/session,” and “close before major news” risk framing in the small-account trading guidance, though that guidance is generic rather than crypto-specific.[2][3][4][6]
  - **Entry rule:** Buy the first pullback to the **20EMA** after a daily breakout, or buy a 2-bar continuation when the intraday high breaks the prior day’s high by more than a small volatility buffer, such as **0.25 ATR**.
  - **Exit rule:** Exit on a close back below the **20EMA** on the chosen timeframe, or at **2R**; use a trailing stop under the prior higher low once price moves **1R** in your favor.
  - **Position sizing:** Risk **0.5%–1% of equity per trade**; for a $500 account, that is **$2.50–$5** max loss, which usually means very small size or waiting for tighter setups.[4][5]
