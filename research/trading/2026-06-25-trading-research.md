### **1. Crypto Momentum with Regime Filters (Pullback Trend + FVG)**
*Best for: Capturing crypto trends without chasing price; avoids FOMO entries.*

- **Time Horizon:** 4H to Daily (swing trades held 2–7 days) [1]
- **Regime Filter:** Only trade when the 200-period EMA is sloping upward AND the 14-period RSI is >50 (bullish regime) [1][2]
- **Entry Rule:** 
  1. Wait for price to pull back into a **Fair Value Gap (FVG)** (a 3-candle imbalance where the middle candle’s high/low doesn’t overlap with the prior/next candle).
  2. Enter on a **bullish confirmation candle** (close above FVG’s high) with volume ≥1.5× average [1][2]
- **Exit Rule:** 
  - Take **50% profit** at the next liquidity pool (previous swing high) [6]
  - Trail the remaining 50% to the next logical resistance level; exit if price closes below the FVG’s low [1][6]
- **Position Sizing:** Start with **1–3% exposure** per trade; max 10% total per asset [2][5]
- **Risk Rule:** Stop-loss placed **below the FVG’s low** (liquidity sweep) [1][6]
- **Source:** "Pullback Trend Strategy with FVG" (YouTube, 2026) [1]; "Momentum Trading Alliance" framework (Buy Support, Not Resistance) [5]
