## 1) Crypto momentum with regime filters

The strongest retail-friendly structure here is **trend-following in liquid crypto only when the higher-timeframe regime is favorable**, using simple filters like moving averages, relative volume, and breakout confirmation.[3][5][9]

**Concrete setup**
- **Universe:** BTC, ETH, and the most liquid large-cap alts only; avoid thin perps and low-liquidity tokens because slippage can erase edge.
- **Time horizon:** **1 day to 2 weeks**.
- **Regime filter:** Trade long only when price is above the **200-day moving average** and the **50-day MA is above the 200-day MA**; optionally require the daily trend to be rising and price to be above VWAP/short-term moving averages on the trigger timeframe.[3][5][8]
- **Momentum trigger:** Enter on a **breakout above the previous 20-day high** or a **pullback-reclaim** of the 20-day/21-day EMA after an impulsive move, with **relative volume > 2.0** when using intraday entries.[5]
- **Entry rule:** Buy only after a daily close above the breakout level, or on a 5–15 minute retest that holds above VWAP for intraday continuation.[3][5]
- **Exit rule:** Use a **2R target** or trail with a **2–3 ATR stop** under the breakout base; if the asset closes back below the 20-day EMA or the 50-day MA in a risk-off regime, exit.[1][3]
- **Position sizing:** Risk **0.25%–1.0% of account equity per trade**; for a $100–$1,000 account that usually means very small notional size, so use only the most liquid instruments and avoid overtrading.[1][4][5]

**Why this is still actionable**
- Trend and breakout systems remain the most straightforward retail systematic edge when costs are low and liquidity is good.[3][8][9]
- The practical edge is not “finding the perfect token”; it is **avoiding trades when the regime is hostile** and using small, repeatable risk.[1][4][5]

**Crowding / degradation risk**
- Intraday VWAP breakout and RVOL-based setups are widely taught and therefore likely the **most crowded** part of this category.[5]
- If you see repeated wick-throughs and failed breakouts, that is usually a sign the momentum edge has **degraded** in the current tape, so reduce size or move to higher-timeframe breakouts only.[5]
