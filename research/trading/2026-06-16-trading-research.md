### 1) Crypto momentum with regime filters

A workable systematic version for a small account is: trade **only in the direction of the higher-timeframe regime**, and use **pullbacks** rather than chasing vertical moves.[1][5] The provided results support the broad idea of momentum/trend-following and pullback entries, including using support/resistance, higher-timeframe structure, and an ATR-like or structure-based stop.[1][2][4][5]

**Concrete parameters**
- **Universe:** 5-15 liquid spot pairs only, avoiding illiquid altcoins.[7]
- **Regime filter:** 50-day price above 200-day moving average on the daily chart, or daily close above a rising 200-day average; only long when regime is positive.[2][5]  
- **Entry:** buy after a pullback to the 20-day EMA or a prior breakout level, *only if* the daily regime remains bullish and 4H momentum turns back up.[1][5]
- **Exit:** initial stop below the recent swing low or below the pullback zone; take partial profits at **2R** and let the rest run to **3R** or a trailing stop under rising swing lows.[1][2][5]
