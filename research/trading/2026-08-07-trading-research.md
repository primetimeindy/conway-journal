## 1) Crypto momentum with regime filters

The cleanest retail implementation is a **daily or 4-hour trend strategy** with a volatility/regime gate, because momentum is easier to automate than most mean-reversion systems and can be sized down to tiny accounts with exchange-native leverage set to zero.[6][17][20]

**Core idea**
- Trade only when the market is in a **trend regime** and avoid chop.
- Use a **higher-timeframe filter** to define direction, then enter on a pullback or breakout continuation.
- Risk a fixed fraction of equity per trade, ideally **0.5%–1%** for a small account.[7][18]

**Concrete parameters**
- **Time horizon:** 1–5 days for swing crypto; 4H entries with daily filter.
- **Regime filter:** Only long when price is above the **200-day EMA** and the 50-day EMA is above the 200-day EMA; only short when the reverse is true. Add a volatility filter such as realized volatility above its 60-day median to avoid dead markets. This is an implementation choice consistent with general trend-following practice, not directly specified in the sources.[6][17][20]
- **Entry rule:** After trend regime is confirmed, enter on a pullback to the **20-day EMA** or the prior breakout level, then require a reclaim of the 5-day high for long entries.
- **Exit rule:** Initial stop below the last swing low or **1.5× ATR(14)**, whichever is tighter; take partial profit at **2R**, trail the rest with a 10-day low or 2×ATR stop.
- **Position sizing:** Risk **0.5%–1% of equity** per trade. On a $500 account, that is $2.50–$5 risk per trade; on a $1,000 account, $5–$10.[7][18]

**Why it is actionable now**
- Retail crypto venues allow fractional sizing and constant monitoring, so the strategy can be run with very small nominal exposure.
- Trend systems are simple enough to automate or follow systematically, which is useful when fees and slippage are meaningful relative to account size.[20]

**Degradation / crowding risk**
- **Moderate degradation risk** in the most obvious large-cap coins and obvious breakout signals, because these are heavily watched.
- If you use this, prefer **less-followed alts with sufficient liquidity** or stick to BTC/ETH only when the regime is strong. That is an inference from market structure; the provided sources do not directly test it.
