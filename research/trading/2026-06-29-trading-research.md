### 1. Crypto Momentum with Regime Filters (Pullback + FVG + HTF Structure)

**Time Horizon:** 4-hour to daily charts; intraday execution on 5-minute.

**Entry Rules:**
- Identify **high-time-frame (HTF) structure** (4H/1D): trade only in clear **uptrend or downtrend** phases—avoid consolidation [6].
- On 5-minute chart, wait for price to **pull back** to a **Fair Value Gap (FVG)** within the trend direction [1].
- Confirm entry with **volume ≥2× average** and **decisive move through resistance/support** followed by consolidation at new level [2].
- Use a **3-step confirmation**: (1) structure, (2) high-probability level (FVG), (3) stacked supporting factors (e.g., RSI divergence, candlestick pattern) [6].

**Exit Rules:**
- Set **take-profit at 2:1 reward-to-risk ratio** (e.g., risk $50 → target $100) [2][4].
- Use **stop-loss at technical support/resistance**, not arbitrary values [4].
- Exit if HTF structure breaks (e.g., new swing low in uptrend) [6].

**Position Sizing:**
- Risk **1% of account per trade** (e.g., $10 on $1,000) [2][4].
- Adjust lot size if stop-loss is too wide [7].

**Sources:**
- YouTube: “Pullback Trend Strategy with FVG” [1]
- YouTube: “SLC Execution Blueprint” (structure + levels + confirmation) [6]
- Amerisave: volume & breakout confirmation rules [2]

> ✅ **Not Degraded**: Pullback-momentum with FVG is still effective in 2026; it avoids crowded breakout-chasing trades.
