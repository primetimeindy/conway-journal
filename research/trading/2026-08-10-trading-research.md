### 1) Crypto momentum with regime filters

The most actionable version for a small account is **spot or perp momentum on liquid majors only** with a **trend filter plus volatility filter**, because raw momentum systems degrade badly in sideways regimes. The provided results do not include a peer-reviewed crypto paper, but they do support the practical components: trend-following, VWAP/EMA structure, RVOL, and disciplined risk sizing are repeatedly emphasized in current retail strategy material[15][18][2].

**Concrete systematic template**
- **Universe:** BTC, ETH, and 1–3 highly liquid large caps only.
- **Time horizon:** **4H to 1D** signals; hold **1–10 days**.
- **Regime filter:** Trade only when **price > 200-day MA** on the daily and **20-day realized volatility is above its 6-month median**; skip when daily price is below the 200-day MA or the 14-day ATR is collapsing.
- **Entry rule:** Enter on a **breakout above the 20-day high** or a **pullback to the 20-day EMA** after breakout confirmation; the 2026 retail materials explicitly recommend trend pullback logic rather than chasing and using structure like EMAs/VWAP/RVOL[15][2][20].
- **Exit rule:**  
  - Initial stop: **1.5 × ATR(14)** below entry for longs.  
  - Take partial profits at **2R**.  
  - Trail remainder with **10-day low** or **2 × ATR** trail.
- **Position sizing:** Risk **0.5%–1.0% of account equity per trade**; with \$100–\$1,000, that means about **\$0.50–\$10 max loss** per trade. The small-account sources repeatedly stress low fixed-risk sizing and avoiding overtrading[6][9][15].

**What likely degraded in the last 6 months**
- **Pure breakout without regime filter** has likely degraded, especially in choppy altcoin tapes, because retail strategy guides now heavily emphasize waiting for confirmation, trend context, and RVOL instead of naked breakouts[15][2].
- **High-frequency intraday momentum** is also a poor fit for tiny accounts because fees and slippage dominate.
