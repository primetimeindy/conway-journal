### 2. Equity Pair Trading (Statistical Arbitrage)
**Limitation for Small Accounts:**
*   **Constraint:** True statistical pair trading (long one asset, short a correlated one) typically requires **margin accounts** and access to **short-selling**, which is often restricted or cost-prohibitive for $100–$1,000 accounts in the US (Pattern Day Trading rules, margin minimums)[11].
*   **Actionable Alternative:** **Sector Rotation** (Momentum style).
    *   **Strategy:** Buy the top decile (strongest) sector ETF and hold; avoid shorting.
    *   **Time Horizon:** Days to weeks (Trend Following)[2][6].
    *   **Entry:** Buy when price breaks above the **50-day SMA** or 200-day SMA (Golden Cross) on SPY or sector ETFs[10].

    *   **Sizing:** 1–2% per trade[10].
*   **Degradation Flag:** Momentum in single-instrument retail trading is **less applicable** than sector rotation; simple trend following remains the "most-profitable systematic style" despite low win rates (~30–45%)[2].
