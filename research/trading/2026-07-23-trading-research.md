## 1) Crypto momentum with regime filters

**Best retail implementation:** trade only the strongest liquid coins and only when the market regime is supportive.

- **Universe:** BTC, ETH, and 1–3 large liquid alts.
- **Time horizon:** **4H to 3D** swing trades.
- **Regime filter:** only take longs when price is above the **200-day moving average** on the daily chart *and* BTC dominance or broad crypto risk is not deteriorating; only take breakouts when BTC is above its medium-term trend. The general idea of using moving averages and momentum filters is consistent with the trading guidance in the provided sources.[3][6]
- **Entry rule:** buy a **pullback to the 20EMA or 50EMA** after a strong impulse, or buy a **breakout to a new local high** if volume expands.
- **Exit rule:** take partial profits at **1.5R–2R**, then trail with a **7%–10% trailing stop** or below the prior swing low; the provided source explicitly mentions a **7% trailing stop** as a risk control.[6]
- **Position sizing:** risk **0.5%–1% of account equity per trade**; for a $100 account, that means $0.50–$1.00 max loss, which usually forces very small size or paper trading until fees/slippage are manageable.

**What to avoid:**  
- **Low-liquidity alt breakouts**; they are where retail momentum strategies are most crowded and most fragile.
