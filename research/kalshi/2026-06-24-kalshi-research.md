## 4. Perplexity Strategy Synthesis
For small accounts ($50–$500) trading on Kalshi and Polymarket in 2026, the most actionable strategies are **convergence plays**, **news-driven arbitrage**, and **tail decay harvesting**, with strict parameters: position size ≤2–5% of capital, trade only when probability shifts ≥10%, and target price gaps of ≥$0.03 net of fees. Cross-venue arbitrage and combinatorial edge cases remain viable but require multi-market monitoring and faster execution.

Below is a concrete breakdown of each strategy with specific parameters:

---

### 1. **Convergence Plays (Near-Resolution Contracts)**
Contracts nearing resolution converge to **$1.00 (Yes)** or **$0.00 (No)**. Highly probable events trading at **$0.85–$0.95** offer stable short-term returns.

- **Time Horizon**: 1–7 days before resolution  
- **Price Threshold**: Buy “Yes” at **$0.90–$0.95**  
- **Position Sizing**: 2–5% of capital per trade  
- **Expected Return**: 5–10% per contract with controlled risk  
- **Why It Works for Small Accounts**: Low volatility, high probability, and predictable payoff  
- **Source**: [5] recommends this as a core starter strategy; [1] notes stable short-term returns on high-probability events.

---

### 2. **Tail Decay Harvesting (Time Decay Trades)**
As contracts age, time decay reduces the value of “out-of-the-money” options. Early in the term, small accounts can buy low-probability “Yes” tickets that decay rapidly if the event becomes unlikely.

- **Time Horizon**: 1–4 weeks before resolution  
- **Price Threshold**: Buy “Yes” at **$0.05–$0.15** on events with <10% implied probability  
- **Position Sizing**: 1–3% per trade (higher risk, lower capital commitment)  
- **Strategy**: Hold until probability drops below 5%, then sell or let expire  
- **Why It Works for Small Accounts**: Low entry cost, high leverage on decay  
- **Source**: [7] (YouTube strategy #1) and [5] list time decay as a foundational play.

---

### 3. **Cross-Venue Arbitrage (Kalshi vs. Polymarket)**
Arbitrage between platforms exploits **2–5% price gaps** on major events. Target gaps of **≥$0.03 net of fees**.

- **Formula**: Buy “Yes” on Platform A + “No” on Platform B. If total cost < $1.00, profit is locked.
- **Example**: Pay $0.97 total → lock in $0.03 profit  
- **Price Threshold**: Act only when gap ≥ $0.03 after fees  
- **Position Sizing**: 2–5% per arb pair  
- **Time Horizon**: Execute within minutes to hours before price corrects  
- **Why It Works for Small Accounts**: Risk-free profit if executed correctly  
- **Source**: [1] confirms 2–5% gaps; [5] and [7] (strategy #3) detail combinatorial arbitrage.
